# How Priority compares with other tools

This is a high-level comparison of each tool's typical role, not an exhaustive feature or pricing audit. Features vary by product plan, institution, and integrations.

| Tool | What it does | Gap for this student workflow | What Priority does differently |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Canvas](https://www.instructure.com/canvas) | Learning management system for courses, materials, assignments, submissions, and grades. | Organizes course activity, but is not centered on combining grade planning with a prioritized daily workload across courses. | Uses Canvas coursework as context for a cross-course Today list and grade planning; Canvas remains the course system. |
| [Todoist](https://todoist.com/features) | General-purpose task lists, projects, due dates, and reminders. | Tasks do not inherently include a student's Canvas assignment details or course-grade calculations. | Connects coursework and grade context to the student's daily work list. |
| [Notion](https://www.notion.com/product) | Flexible workspace for documents, databases, projects, and tasks. | Students must create or adapt a workspace for coursework; it is not purpose-built around Canvas assignments and grade calculations. | Provides a ready-made student workflow that brings coursework, grades, and daily priorities together. |
| [Grade-calculator sites](https://www.calculator.net/grade-calculator.html) | Calculate a current or target grade from scores and weights entered by the student. | Usually answer a focused calculation; they do not manage the assignments and next actions behind the grade. | Connects grade calculations with course assignments and daily priorities, reducing the need to move between a calculator and a task list. |
| [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/overview/) | Conversational AI for explanations, questions, and general-purpose help. | Unless a student supplies the relevant course information, a chat does not inherently reflect their current assignments, grades, or syllabus. | Combines student coursework and grade calculations with answers grounded in saved course syllabi. |

Priority is intended to complement a learning management system, not replace course delivery or submission tools.
